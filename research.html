Research Agenda

From representation learning to risk stratification.

Current projects focus on multimodal machine learning for Parkinson's disease, including deep learning, representation learning, and clinically useful prediction across high-dimensional biomedical data.

Supervisors: Dr Cynthia Sandor, Prof Payam Barnaghi, Dr Pedro Mediano, Dr Yen Foung Tai
Chapter 1 · Complete

Proteomics-based Parkinson's prediction

Machine learning models trained on UK Biobank plasma proteomics data across more than 52,000 participants, followed by cross-cohort validation on PPMI with SHAP analysis and age-stratified calibration.

Chapter 2 · In Progress

Multimodal EHR and proteomics fusion

Deep learning architectures that combine structured electronic healthcare records with proteomic features, using representation learning to improve subgroup discovery, predictive performance, and interpretability.

Supervision

Sleep, proteomics, and Parkinson's disease risk

Investigating whether sleep disturbances contribute to Parkinson's risk through proteomic and digital biomarker pathways, using UK Biobank, Mendelian randomisation, and causal machine learning approaches.

Supervision

Transcriptomics in REM behavioural disorder

Supervising work on whole blood transcriptome profiling and bulk RNA-seq pipelines to quantify alpha-synucleinopathic phenoconversion risk.

Supervision

Single-nucleus analysis of motor cortex in Parkinson's disease

Exploring single-nucleus transcriptomic signatures in the primary motor cortex to identify disease-relevant cell populations and molecular programs.

Methods

Core technical capabilities.

Clinical proteomics

Targeted proteomics platforms including Olink and SomaLogic are used to define predictive signatures and mechanistic leads.

Representation learning

Latent feature learning across structured and multimodal patient data to capture pre-diagnostic patterns, comorbidity structure, and clinical trajectories.

Deep learning and digital biomarkers

Deep learning pipelines for smartwatch and remote-monitoring data support continuous behavioural and physiological phenotyping outside clinic walls.
