About

Applying advanced ML to neurodegenerative disease — from proteomics and wearables to interpretable biomarker discovery.

Marirena Bafaloukou is a Research Assistant for Machine Learning for Clinical Data Science at the Brain Sciences Department UK Dementia Research Institute at Imperial College London, where she is also pursuing a PhD in Clinical Medicine Research.

Her doctoral work focuses on developing and applying advanced machine learning methods to identify predictive and stratification biomarkers for Parkinson's Disease using clinical proteomics, electronic healthcare record data, and smartwatch data. She works with large-scale datasets including the UK Biobank and the PPMI cohort for cross-cohort validation and biomarker discovery.

Her earlier research involved leveraging remote monitoring data to build interpretable tools for detecting and managing agitation episodes in patients with dementia — work published in eClinicalMedicine and Alzheimer's & Dementia.

Current Research
Chapter 1 · Complete

Proteomics-Based Parkinson's Prediction

ML models on UK Biobank plasma proteomics (>52,000 participants), ~0.73 ROC-AUC. Cross-cohort validation on PPMI with SHAP analysis and age-stratified calibration.

Chapter 2 · In Progress

Multimodal EHR + Proteomics Fusion

Deep learning architectures integrating electronic heathcare records and proteomics, with a focus on model interpretability.

Supervision

Transcriptomics in REM Behavioural Disorder

Supervising projects on whole blood transcriptome profiling and bulk RNA-Seq workflows to measure alpha-synucleinopathic phenoconversion risk.

Ongoing

Single-Nucleus Analysis of Motor Cortex in PD

Exploring single-nucleus transcriptomic signatures in the primary motor cortex of Parkinson's Disease patients to identify disease-relevant cell populations.
