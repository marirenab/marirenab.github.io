Profile

Machine learning for high-value biomedical data.

Her work focuses on production-minded modelling problems in healthcare data: multimodal prediction, clinical stratification, deep learning architectures, and representation learning for complex patient signals. Earlier projects in dementia monitoring produced interpretable models for remote sensing and longitudinal behavioural data.

Machine learning across proteomics, wearables, and health records, with an emphasis on models that can generalize, explain, and transfer.
